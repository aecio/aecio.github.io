# About Me

Hey! 👋🏽 I’m Aécio (pronounced as in: *ah-eh-see-oh*).

##### Professional Experience

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Database Architectures group at [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica (CWI)](https://www.cwi.nl/en/) in Amsterdam. Previously, I was a Research Engineer at New York University (NYU) in New York City, where I worked on multiple research and development programs, including DARPA’s Memex, D3M, ASKEM and ARPAH-H's Biomedical Data Fabric Toolbox. Before that, I worked as a Software Engineer at a Brazilian start-up called [Zunnit Technologies](https://web.archive.org/web/20150226205818/http://www.zunnit.com/index_en.html), where we built a scalable news recommendation platform used by major Brazilian media companies.


##### Education

I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science from New York University's Tandon School of Engineering, advised by Prof. Juliana Freire. Throughout my Ph.D., I've been fortunate to have been mentored by [Chris Musco](https://www.chrismusco.com/) and [Flip Korn](https://research.google/people/106040/). Previously, I received a Master's Degree in Computer Science from the [Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)](http://dcc.ufmg.br/dcc/), and a *Technology Degree* in Analysis and Development of Systems from [Federal Institute of Piauí (IFPI)](http://ifpi.edu.br/).

##### Research Experience

I've had the opportunity to work in several research projects which resulted in various <a href="{{ site.base_url }}/publications">publications</a> in top-tier research venues, which include *VLDB*, *SIGMOD*, *ICDE*, *PODS*, *The Web Conference (WWW)*, *SIGIR*, *WSDM*, *CIKM*, *SPIRE*, and *Information Retrieval Journal*. I'm interested in solving complex problems (which usually involve dealing with large amounts of data) that lie in the intersection of research areas such as *Information Retrieval*, *Machine Learning*, *Data Management*, and *Information Visualization*.
